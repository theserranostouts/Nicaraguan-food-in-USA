The Story Behind the Book

Food for My Love began as a deeply personal gift from John Stout to his husband, Yonathan Serrano.

John created the cookbook to celebrate Yonathan’s Nicaraguan heritage and to honor the food, memories, and traditions that connect him to home.

More than a collection of recipes, this project represents love, family, cultural pride, and the way food can keep a place close to the heart, even while living far away.

Nicaragua: The Heart of the Cookbook

Nicaragua’s culinary traditions are shaped by family, regional ingredients, Indigenous heritage, and generations of shared meals.

Preserving a Connection to Home

For Yonathan, these recipes are more than instructions for preparing food. They are reminders of family, community, and the country where his story began.

Food for My Love carries those traditions into a new chapter of life in the United States.

John Stout and Yonathan Serrano together outdoors

About John & Yonathan

Food for My Love reflects a shared life built around love, cultural appreciation, family, and the belief that traditions become stronger when they are preserved and shared.

“John, thank you for creating this cookbook for me and for embracing the food and culture of Nicaragua with so much love. This gift makes me feel seen, valued, and connected to home. I will always treasure what you created for us.”
