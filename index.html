John created the cookbook to celebrate Yonathan’s Nicaraguan heritage and to honor the food, memories, and traditions that connect him to home. More than a collection of recipes, this project represents love, family, cultural pride, and the way food can keep a place close to the heart, even while living far away. ♥ “May these recipes always bring you closer to home.” From John to Yonathan 📖 82 Recipes A broad collection of traditional Nicaraguan dishes. 🇳🇮 Nicaraguan Heritage Recipes that preserve culture, memory, and family tradition. ♥ Created with Love A personal gift created by John for Yonathan. Nicaragua: The Heart of the Cookbook Nicaragua’s culinary traditions are shaped by family, regional ingredients, Indigenous heritage, and generations of shared meals.
