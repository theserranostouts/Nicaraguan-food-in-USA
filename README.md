# Food for My Love

A digital collection of authentic Nicaraguan recipes celebrating family, tradition, and the rich flavors of Nicaragua.

## Website

Visit the published website:

https://theserranostouts.github.io/Nicaraguan-food-in-USA/

## About the Project

This project was created to preserve and share traditional Nicaraguan recipes with family, friends, and future generations.

## Contents

- Traditional Nicaraguan recipes
- Digital cookbook in PDF format
- Recipe website
- Family food traditions
